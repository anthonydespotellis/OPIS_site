What is the Solar System?

When we look up at the night sky, we see thousands of stars. A star is a giant, hot ball of gas that makes its own light and heat. The Sun is a star, and it is the only star in our Solar System.

The Sun, and everything that travels around it, is called the Solar System. That includes Earth and seven other planets, along with moons, asteroids and other objects. The Sun sits at the centre, and its pull keeps the planets travelling around it.

In order from the Sun, the eight planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune.
