SECTION 1 — WHAT IS THE SOLAR SYSTEM?
============================================================

Heading
What is the Solar System?

Body copy
When we look up at the night sky, we see thousands of stars. A star is a giant, hot ball of gas that makes its own light and heat. The Sun is a star, and it is the only star in our Solar System.

The Sun, and everything that travels around it, is called the Solar System. That includes Earth and seven other planets, along with moons, asteroids and other objects. The Sun sits at the centre, and its pull keeps the planets travelling around it.

In order from the Sun, the eight planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune. They can be sorted into three groups:

Rocky planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars are made mostly of rock and metal.

Gas planets: Jupiter and Saturn are made mostly of gas.

Ice planets: Uranus and Neptune are made mostly of icy materials.

MNEMONIC CALLOUT
============================================================

Heading
A trick for remembering the order

Body copy
It can be hard to remember the order of the planets. A mnemonic can help. A mnemonic is a phrase where the first letter of each word matches the first letter of each planet, in order from the Sun. For example:

My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Nachos

Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.
